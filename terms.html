02What the service does

ILMA.AI is an automation pipeline that, on a schedule:

  • collects publicly available trending topics from open sources (Reddit, RSS feeds, Hacker News, and similar);
  • generates a short script and voice-over for a selected topic;
  • assembles a short vertical video with captions;
  • publishes that video to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API, and/or to Instagram through Instagram's official API with Instagram Login, only when explicitly enabled by the operator for that platform.

Publishing is disabled by default, per platform. No step in this pipeline scrapes TikTok or Instagram, automates account logins, or bypasses either platform's own rules — every publish goes through that platform's official API.
