02What the service does

ILMA.AI is an automation pipeline that, on a schedule:

  • collects publicly available trending topics from open sources (Reddit, RSS feeds, Hacker News, and similar);
  • generates a short script and voice-over for a selected topic;
  • assembles a short vertical video with captions;
  • publishes that video to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API, only when explicitly enabled by the operator.

Publishing is disabled by default. No step in this pipeline scrapes TikTok, automates account logins, or bypasses TikTok's own rules — every publish goes through TikTok's official API.
