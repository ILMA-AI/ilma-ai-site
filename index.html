Automated trend media

Trending topics, turned into short videos, on autopilot.

ILMA.AI is a solo-built pipeline that detects what's trending, writes and voices a short script, assembles a vertical video, and publishes it — through TikTok's and Instagram's official publishing APIs. Built and operated by one person, from one computer.

What it does

Every few hours, ILMA.AI scans public, open trend sources, scores each topic for how likely it is to resonate, and — for whichever topic clears the bar — writes a short script, generates a voice-over, assembles a vertical video with captions, and publishes it to the platforms that are connected and enabled. Nothing publishes unless the operator has explicitly turned that platform on. Read the full breakdown on the How it works page.

Where it publishes

TikTok

Published through TikTok's official Content Posting API (Direct Post). No scraping, no unofficial automation of the TikTok app or website. The operator authorizes ILMA.AI with their own TikTok account through TikTok's own Login Kit consent screen — Connect with TikTok starts that same login flow.

Instagram

Published as Reels through the Instagram API with Instagram Login (Meta's official Graph API for content publishing).

Why it exists

ILMA.AI started as a test of a simple question: can one person, on one computer, with a zero-budget stack of free and open tools, run a content pipeline end to end — sourcing, writing, voicing, editing and publishing — without violating a single platform's terms of service? Read the full story.
